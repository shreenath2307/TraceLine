FAQ

Common questions

How many anchors do I need?

It depends on floor area and layout. The placement optimizer simulates options and shows the expected error for each, so you can choose the smallest count that meets your accuracy target.

Which anchor hardware is supported?

The method works from range or signal-strength measurements, so it is designed to work with common Bluetooth and UWB anchors through measurement adapters.

What accuracy can I expect?

Accuracy varies with anchor density, noise and obstructions. We report results on hidden test data and recommend a surveyed pilot to confirm it in your building.

What happens when a wall blocks the signal?

Likely obstructed readings are down-weighted, alternative anchors are used, and motion history helps hold the track. The reported uncertainty increases so you can see it.

Can it run without internet?

Yes. Inference can run on a local gateway inside your network.

Is personal data stored?

We track devices through pseudonymous identifiers and keep only what is needed. See the Security page for details.

How long does a pilot take?

Typically a few weeks: survey, anchor setup, calibration walk and a measured comparison.

How were the estimated prices worked out?

They assume a 2 BHK of about 900 sq ft with 4 anchors and 2 tags, or a 3 BHK of about 1,350 sq ft with 6 anchors and 3 tags, plus installation, calibration and software. Your quote replaces them once we see your floor plan.
