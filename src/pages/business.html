Scalability

Why the model scales.

Software-first

The core is an inference service, not custom hardware. It works with commonly available Bluetooth and UWB anchors.

Lower cost per site

Fewer anchors for a given accuracy reduces capital cost and installation time, the two largest barriers to adoption.

Repeatable onboarding

Floor plan upload, anchor survey and calibration walk follow the same steps in every building; the error map then learns the site.

Edge or cloud

Inference can run on a local gateway for latency and privacy, or centrally for multi-site analytics.

Cumulative advantage

Each site adds to our library of obstruction and noise patterns, improving robustness elsewhere.

Adjacent uses

Placement planning and stress testing are valuable on their own to integrators and facility consultants.

Go-to-market

Phased growth.

Pilot

One hospital wing or warehouse zone; measure error against a surveyed reference.

Site rollout

Full-facility deployment with emergency mode and error-map learning.

Multi-site

Chains and groups, integrator partnerships, centralised analytics.

Key risks

  • Accuracy differs between real venues and simulations; pilots are essential
  • Hardware diversity across anchor vendors
  • Privacy expectations in healthcare

Mitigations

  • Pilot with surveyed ground truth
  • Vendor-neutral measurement adapters
  • Privacy-by-design architecture, see Security
